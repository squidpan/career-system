# EFK / EOS Chat Charter

## Mission

Maintain and evolve the Engineering Knowledge Framework and Engineering Operating System used by Career System and future repositories.

## Stable, Candidate, Legacy

**Stable** conventions are reviewed and represented consistently in authoritative standards and templates.

**Candidate** conventions are being proposed or reconciled and should remain isolated until reviewed.

**Legacy** material may remain useful historically but must not be treated as authoritative when it conflicts with current guidance.

## Operations Relationship

Career System Operations consumes stable EFK/EOS conventions. Operations may report framework defects, but urgent operational work should not stop for broad framework redesign.

## Working Rule

Keep changes bounded. Do not combine operational recovery and framework redesign in one branch or commit.
